# 02.04.13 2026-09-28 — Numbers, ML and the library

A numeric type is a *representation* of a mathematical object plus a rounding model; the
specification is the real (or rational) object, and a representation discharges the obligations of
the specification with its own error bound. One rule generalises everything here. It is the
numbers section of the [substrate](02.04.04-substrate.md) taken to its consequences, and it feeds
the reproducible-by-default stance of [principles](02.04.07-principles-and-privileges.md) (P3).

## 1 The representation lattice

Four representations on two axes: **binary floating** (IEEE 754; relative error; dynamic range
free; non-associative); **binary fixed** (scaled integers; absolute error; range is bookkeeping;
associative, reproducible: von Neumann's side of the 1946 argument, right about hidden scale and
error, wrong about convenience, which is a dispensatie not a default); **decimal floating** (IEEE
754-2008 decimal; exact decimal fractions, so `0.1 + 0.2` holds); **decimal fixed** (scaled
integers with a base-10 scale; exact and reproducible; the money representation). Crockford's
critiques of binary floats return as representations, and his "one number type" is answered
better: one specification with representations chosen per obligation.

**`FPL_smooth` stays derived.** A word is in `FPL_smooth` iff its primitives' derivative obligations
close without `sorry` at the level of the type's *denotational model* (not "the real model": an
angle's model is ℝ·turn, ℂ's is ℂ with holomorphic as a further obligation, a Clifford algebra's
its own). A representation adds obligations (an error bound, reproducibility) on their own rungs and
never subtracts membership: a fixed-point `sin` whose CORDIC bound is open is a smooth word with an
open representation obligation. Whether a word is a Real word represented in ℤ or an Int word is in
its type.

**Money.** `Money = Int × Currency`: the amount a scaled decimal integer in minor units with the
scale from ISO 4217 per currency (EUR 2, JPY 0, KWD 3), the currency a tag (`#EUR`). Addition only
within one currency, else a type error; multiplication by a scalar carries a rounding policy as an
obligation (banker's rounding, or Fowler's allocate that loses no cent); division never silent;
exchange is an *effect*: a rate is an oracle, hence a capability with rate and time in the log; the
double-entry invariant (debits equal credits) is a contract, proved at rung 1 where a ledger kernel
exists and checked with blame at rung 3 ([contracts](02.04.11-contracts-cost-totality.md)); ISO
20022 is a foreign theory with a seam.

## 2 Angles and hypercomplex algebras

**Angles: the turn is the unit, `Angle` is a type, τ is derived.** The primitive is the circle
group, not a constant. `turn : -- Angle` is one full turn, an angle is a fraction of it (`1/4
turn`), radians are a projection, `tau : -- Real` the radian measure of one turn and `pi` that of a
half turn, both library words. `sin` and `cos` take an `Angle`, not a `Real`: an angle is
dimensionless but not typeless, and a degree where a radian was expected does not typecheck. Where a
radian constant is needed, τ over π: the period of sin and cos is one turn; the Fourier kernel and
Cauchy's formula lose their 2π; the normalised Gaussian gives √τ; `½τr²` is a quadratic form like
`½mv²`; `ζ(2n) = |B₂ₙ| τ²ⁿ/(2·(2n)!)` is the general law of which π²/6 is a small-number accident.
A ½ in a formula means a half is happening, and with `Angle` the notation says `1/2 turn`.
*Fixed point:* an integer modulo 2^k with overflow as the circle is exactly U(1) (binary angular
measurement as in DSPs), the default representation of rotations; `Angle` proper keeps the winding
number, represented as ℤ[1/2^k]·turn (an integer part of turns plus a k-bit fraction). Then `exp :
Angle -- U(1)` is truncation to the low k bits, exact and free, `arg : U(1) -- Angle` is the identity
on the fraction with the branch choosing the integer part (0 by default, a declared choice as for
branch cuts); sin and cos come from CORDIC in the integers. Radians appear nowhere in the
representation.

**Hypercomplex numbers: no algebra is primitive.** A complex number is ℝ² plus a chosen product: an
algebra. Array languages make ℂ an atom and so lose rank polymorphism over it and stop at ℂ; here the
algebra is data and the product is array arithmetic. Two representations, both existing machinery:
(i) the regular representation (multiplication by a fixed element is linear, so every
finite-dimensional *associative* algebra is a matrix algebra: ℂ as `[[a,−b],[b,a]]`, ℍ as 4×4 real;
Frobenius's boundary is the boundary of the matrix product); (ii) structure constants (a rank-3
tensor `c` with `eᵢeⱼ = Σₖ cᵢⱼₖ eₖ`, the product one contraction, which also covers the
non-associative octonions; Hurwitz). Laws are obligations on the tensor, on a rung: associativity of
ℂ closes with `⊢`; for 𝕆 the hole stays open, which is the truth. The Cayley–Dickson ladder is the
wrong ladder; **Clifford `Cl(p,q,r)`** is the right one: dimension 2ⁿ, associative for every n, ℂ =
Cl(0,1), ℍ = Cl(0,2), dual numbers (forward-mode autodiff) = Cl(0,0,1), split-complex = Cl(1,0),
rotations in any dimension as rotors; a multivector is an array of length 2ⁿ indexed by subsets of
basis vectors. Angle and ℂ are not two things: `exp` maps `Angle` onto U(1), `arg` is the log with a
declared representative. `Complex : Ring → Ring` is a functor, so ℚ[i] is exact, and complex over
intervals and over duals (complex-step differentiation) come free. Holomorphic is a stronger
obligation on the same `d`, not another fragment. Cost: a 2ⁿ×2ⁿ×2ⁿ table is large and mostly zero,
but static, so staging folds the generic contraction back to the hand-written `(a+bi)(c+di)`.

**The scalar ring is an explicit parameter** of the `algebra/` library: structure constants of ℂ, ℍ,
𝕆, Cl are 0 and ±1, so with fixed-point scalars and widening on multiply the laws that hold are
integer identities and discharge *exactly* in ℤ[1/2^k]; with rounding, with an absolute bound; with
floats, relative. What discharges exactly for 𝕆 is alternativity; associativity of 𝕆 is false in
every ring, so that hole is mathematical, not representational.

**Index origin** is a privilege default (`⎕IO`), layout lives on the residual, ℕ's zero is neither
([principles](02.04.07-principles-and-privileges.md)). The BLAS/LAPACK off-by-one is Fortran's
inheritance against NumPy's; APL showed it is a *setting*, per scope. Dijkstra's case for zero
(half-open intervals compose without ±1) is the default. Origins are inductive families `From o`
with `base` and `suc`, so `From 0 ≃ ℕ` and `From 1` is what mathematicians count with; `⎕IO` is which
family a privilege's prelude mounts; a LAPACK jet translates origin and layout once as a bijection.
SaC's tensor comprehensions are a *view* over the combinators under `under sac`.

## 3 ML: integer-first, bit-exact

**FPL as an ML workbench.** The connectionist side (differentiable arrays with shapes in types;
`grad`/`vmap`/`jit` as level-1 functions on code, JAX the existence proof; a world model as a
coalgebra `X → prediction × X`) and the symbolist side (symbolic computation at level 1, logic with
spaces, a `facts/` tree, identity `$`, the ABT as variables-and-binding made data) are present under
other names. What neither gets elsewhere is the *seam* between them, typed: provenance semirings
(Scallop, egglog) make a Datalog derivation differentiable so a gradient crosses a symbolic step;
the differentiable side is an effect encoding, the logical side an embedding, the semiring the
morphism. Around it the workbench re-derived: tracking is the log, a sweep is a fork, a model
registry is `$`/`&`, a dataset is a segment with capabilities *and* an information-flow grade, a
notebook a level-1 program with a residual, inference bit-reproducible by integer computation. Gaps
before "lingua franca" is honest: training reproducibility; a tensor compiler; and the world (PyTorch,
CUDA, datasets), wrapped behind FFI capabilities with `sorry`s. Whether it becomes a lingua franca is
a social fact no design produces.

**Integer (fixed-point) training is the reproducible default; float training runs under
dispensatie.** A lineage (DSPs; integer training and inference; NITI; INT8 training; ternary
weights). Determinism comes not from "integer" but from *associativity*: integer addition is
associative, so a parallel reduction gives the same bits whatever the lane order, provided
accumulators widen instead of saturating; float fails on non-associativity, not precision. Training
needs dynamic range (per-tensor or per-block scales, block floating point) and stochastic rounding,
which needs a seeded, logged PRNG capability. Costs: accuracy and stability on some models; no
hardware advantage today. Fixed point is not a subset of `FPL_smooth` but a representation: ℝ is the
specification, float and fixed point are two machine representations, and fixed point closes two
obligations float cannot, reproducibility and an *absolute* error bound (which composes additively;
interval arithmetic is trivial). Float training is permitted as a `+nefas` region under
`../adyton/…`, the log marking it non-replayable. This reverses the industry default.

**Batch invariance is schedule purity.** From the Thinking Machines study of nondeterminism in LLM
inference: the nondeterminism is not atomics (the forward pass has none) but a lack of *batch
invariance*, the reduction order depending on batch size, hence on server load: a request's result
depends on an input that is not its own. The fix is kernels whose reduction order is a function of
the element, not the batch (about 20% cost). Correction to the above: the culprit is float
non-associativity under *varying* order. Two routes to one property: fix the order (float stays) or
make the operation associative (integer). In FPL's terms a *schedule* (a level-1 program) may not
read the batch size: purity by absence applied to the schedule, static not a test suite. Version
invariance is the kernel being `&`-keyed with a specified reference inference. Bitwise determinism,
distributional reproducibility and semantic stability map to replay, a PRNG capability with a
logged seed, and contracts: determinism of the *program* does not force determinism of the
*distribution*, it makes it replayable.

**The tensor stack short-circuited.** Following tinygrad (the ML stack is ten thousand lines, not ten
million): (1) a small lazy op set as the array theory (elementwise, reduce, movement: reshape,
permute, expand, pad; matmul derived), every model a graph over it; (2) fusion is equality
saturation (Tensat), i.e. the e-graph over a declared theory with extraction by a cost model; (3)
schedules are level-1 programs, written or searched, cached by hash; (4) batch invariance is
schedule purity; (5) the small thing, codegen per accelerator from the op set, a few hundred lines
per target. The performance tail (FlashAttention-class kernels) becomes *jets*. Models are weights
plus a graph: weights are `&` segments, the graph ONNX, a foreign theory with a one-way embedding
written once as a functor; CUDA is a backend plus jets behind a capability with translation
validation (numeric for float, exact for integer), never a dependency; optimisers and training loops
are a hundred lines, re-derived. **The lever:** integer is the reproducible path, integer kernels
are simpler than bf16 tensor-core kernels and run on commodity CPUs, so the reproducible default
needs no CUDA. Oracles for differential testing: micrograd for autodiff, tinygrad for the op set.
**T4 is the op set as kernel plus the rank calculus as derived theorems**, not fenced: `f each` over
the leading axis is expand-the-frame plus elementwise; `fold` and `scan` are reduce and scan along
an axis; leading-axis agreement (APL) and right-aligned broadcasting (NumPy) are both views over the
op set because `expand` takes an explicit shape (the surface follows the array tradition: leading).
SaC's shape classes (known shape, known dimensionality, unknown) are the gradual stratification
shapes-in-types need, specialised by staging.

## 4 The standard library

**Data structures, four layers.** *Shape*: every inductive structure is the initial algebra of a
polynomial functor (containers), every coinductive one the final coalgebra; list, tree, heap, trie
are one mechanism; indexing by ornaments. *Laws*: the Boom hierarchy (tree, list by associativity,
bag by commutativity, set by idempotence) as declared theories for the e-graph, so a set is a list
plus two rewrite rules; in the cubical universe the same hierarchy is a family of quotient HITs
(finite multisets and finite sets), the e-graph being the strict-universe shadow
([the plane](02.04.15-meta-level-and-plane.md)). *Representation*: separate from shape (AoS/SoA,
layout, residency), chosen on the residual; Okasaki for the persistent case, with uniqueness giving
in-place reuse so one definition covers persistent and imperative performance. *Cost and proof*:
amortised bounds are cost grades; every entry carries `contract`, `proof`, `cost` as directory
children ([contracts](02.04.11-contracts-cost-totality.md)). **Algorithms** are recursion schemes
(folds, unfolds, hylomorphisms) with fusion laws as the optimisations; the array combinators are
those schemes already.

**Domains, by the re-derivation rule** (re-derive the semantics, wrap the world;
[history](02.04.10-history-store-distribution.md)):

| Domain | Treatment |
| --- | --- |
| numerics, linear algebra | op set and algebras; BLAS/LAPACK as jets |
| text and Unicode | strings as codepoint arrays; regex by Brzozowski derivatives (provable); ICU wrapped for normalisation and collation, never re-derived |
| parsing | Lark as reference; combinators and Earley as schemes |
| serialisation | the three sigils; CBOR on the wire |
| cryptography | *never* re-derived: blake3, ed25519, libsodium behind capabilities with `sorry` |
| networking and time | channels, the log and the time capability; TLS wrapped |
| graphs and geometry | functors and Clifford algebras |
| probability | the Giry monad as an effect |
| compression, image, sound | wrapped until re-derived |

Layout `/fpl/lib/<domain>/` with the same conventions everywhere.

**`/fpl/lib/comb` (alias `aviary`).** Smullyan's birds as petnames over the stack combinators:
`drop` the Kestrel (K), `swap` the Cardinal (C), `dup` the Warbler-like W, `!` the Idiot (I),
composition the Bluebird (B); `dip`, `sip`, `cons` are Kerby's concatenative basis. The bases sort by
grade: BCI is the *linear* basis (Church's λI), BCK the *affine*, and only S, W, M need ω, so which
birds a linear scope may use is fixed by the grade lattice ([syntax rationale](02.04.05-syntax-rationale.md)).
The Sage bird, Y, exists in a total language only under `+div` or `recur`: the one bird needing a
word on the effect line. Beyond Smullyan: Curry–Feys (Curry's paradox is why M stays untyped),
Turner's SK machine and supercombinators (the compilation story for the residual), Curien's
categorical combinators (the Lambek reading), Lafont's interaction combinators (the backend for the
dup-free fragment).

**`/fpl/lib/cat` (petname `nlab`): the language's structural spine.** A univalent category-theory
library in the cubical universe, the test case of the first milestone and the place the rest gets
its structure: monads (`/fpl/lib/cat/mon/giry` for probability), containers and functors,
algebras, coalgebras are defined there once; the effect encodings of the seam table are its Kleisli
constructions. Its code counterparts are 1Lab, agda-unimath, agda-categories, mathlib (check
licences before taking anything). Philosophically: ontic structural realism holds that what exists
are structures; univalence is the structure identity principle (isomorphic structures are
identical), so a univalent library makes structural realism *executable*. The honest caveat: the
substrate itself is not structural: `&` is identity of syntax modulo alpha, `$` identity of birth;
structural realism holds *inside* the univalent universe as a library, not beneath it. C1's three
identities are the three positions: syntactic (`&`), modulo a theory (the e-graph), structural
(bisimulation; with univalence, equivalence); the third is the maintainer's commitment, the
platform makes it computable and stands on the first. The synthetic viewpoint (define by universal
property, not construction) is the house style. A strict (UIP) category library lives at the meta
level early ([compiler](02.04.12-compiler-backends-assurance.md), K7); the univalent one in the
cubical universe, R8 the seam.
