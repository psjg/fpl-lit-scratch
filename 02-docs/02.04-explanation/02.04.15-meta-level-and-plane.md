## 1 Quotations, stages and reflection

One notation `[ ]`, two types, classified by consumer: `!` makes a thunk `U B` at level 0, `¨`,
`( )` or catenation onto code make it `Code B` at level 1; a quotation both forced and inspected in
one frame is an error. `Code` equality is hash equality (stored identity at level 1); thunk equality
is behavioural; nothing at level 0 distinguishes quotations by text, which is the guarantee Wand's
result leaves available. The level boundary sits at `:`/`bind`, exactly on C1's identity boundary.
`lift`, `reify` and `reflect` are behind `../meta` and *stage-creating*: `code ../meta eval-with`
runs `Code` under a new evaluator rather than mutating the running one (Brown's tower properly); the
tower is history in the log, never simultaneously live levels. At level 0 a thunk runs in the
environment it was closed over, never one supplied by the caller. The keys that unlock the
forbidden powers are in [principles](02.04.07-principles-and-privileges.md). Classification across
frames (a quotation returned and consumed differently by two callers) is open: likely fixed at the
first consumer.

## 2 Macros are level-1 functions

A macro is an ordinary level-1 function `Code → Code` (an `each` over a token array), not a special
form: no `defmacro`, `syntax-rules` or quasiquote construct. **Hygiene is on two layers**: over the
token array a macro is as unhygienic as `defmacro` (the open nested-`→x` question); over the ABT it
is hygienic by construction because binding is structure. Rule: macros are written over the ABT;
the token layer is the reader layer. **Hygiene is a scope capability, not a flag**: hygienic
(default) bindings land in a fresh child scope of the call site; unhygienic writes into the
*caller's* scope, which requires a capability on that scope, given by the caller passing the scope
as an argument (Racket's `datum->syntax` and the anaphoric `aif` are the precedents; from recall).
**Monkeypatching** is a union mount ([objects](02.04.09-objects-dispatch-visibility.md)). **Macros
are pure and hermetic**: expansion is staging, and staging without capabilities is pure, so a macro
that reads a file needs a capability and `run` from `../adyton` is the key for unrestricted.
**Expansion is memoised on hash** and carries source positions. **Fresh names** are derived from
(macro `&`, call site, ordinal), identical at every peer, because a global gensym counter breaks
determinism; over the ABT a fresh binder needs no name; `$` stays reserved for birth identity.

## 3 Two kinds of rewriting

The language is symbolic at level 1 and homoiconic, so arbitrary rewriting exists, in two kinds
Wolfram merges. **Rewriting as computation**: rules as `match` agendas over `Code` with an explicit
strategy (outermost or innermost, once, to a fixed point); arbitrary, neither confluent nor
terminating, no claim of equality; a fixed-point loop without fuel carries `+div`. **Rewriting as
equality**: the e-graph over a declared theory, where every rule must be a proved equality because
the optimiser extracts by cost; putting unrestricted rules in is how a compiler becomes unsound. A
rule starts as a computation and becomes an equality only with a proof or a contract.

**The proof is a bisimulation.** Over the system coalgebra `Fsys X = Act → List(Obs × X)` with X
the configurations (stack, code), a rule `L → R` is an equality iff `L` and `R` stay bisimilar in
every context. Two conditions. *Congruence*: bisimilarity must be a congruence, else rewriting inside
a term is illegitimate (the e-graph does it constantly); for higher-order languages this is
environmental bisimulation (Sangiorgi–Kobayashi–Sumii; congruence without Howe's method; Sumii–Pierce
on dynamic sealing is the precedent for sealing). *What is observable*: for pure terms only the
result (observational equality); for terms with capabilities the effect events in the log, so a rule
is an equality iff `L` and `R` emit the same event sequence (replay-equal); with nondeterminism
bisimulation is the default and trace equivalence a deliberately weaker declared theory. This closes
C1: identity 2 (e-graph modulo a declared theory) is justified by identity 3 (behaviour); a theory is
correct iff every axiom is a bisimulation. Rung 1 is a bisimulation proof in the atlas, rung 2
generates contexts in which the walker runs `L` and `R` and compares event sequences, rung 3 a
contract at a hole. Feasible for the pure first-order fragment; higher-order with effects is
research level. The seam to the cubical universe: the e-graph is the strict-universe shadow of a
quotient HIT, and what is shadowed is the *set truncation*; the statement to prove is that
saturation over the declared theory computes a canonical representative of the 0-truncated HIT,
rung 1 for the HIT and rung 2/3 for the e-graph, so a theory outside the confluent-and-terminating
domain has a HIT but no complete shadow, which is a hole and should be one
([numbers and library](02.04.13-numbers-ml-library.md)).

## 4 FPL is symbolic by tier

At level 1 everything "symbolic" means exists as a first-class thing: expressions as data (a
quotation consumed by `¨`, `( )` or catenation is `Code`; Wolfram's `Hold` is the default status of
any quotation not forced); symbols as paths and identities; rewriting as the e-graph over a declared
theory (Wolfram's `Flat` and `Orderless` are associativity and commutativity, equality saturation
modulo AC, and `Simplify` with rules is saturation with a theory); definitions as rewrite rules on
patterns as `match` agendas over `Code` with exhaustiveness as holes; exact arithmetic as the
dyadic/rational representation; symbolic differentiation as a level-1 library beside automatic
differentiation as a level-0 effect encoding (two seams, both present). At level 0 it is not
symbolic: Wolfram's real choice is that everything is an expression at run time, ambient reflection,
hence Wand, hence no type system and a residual that is an interpreter. FPL stages instead: after
staging the code is flat. Whoever holds `../adyton/reflect` (`+nefas`) gets run-time symbolic
computation back, recorded as a dispensatie. A computer-algebra system in FPL is a program that lives
at level 1 and exports a REPL; the rest of the language does not pay its price.

## 5 The plane

**2LTT.** Annenkov, Capriotti, Kraus, Sattler: an inner theory (HoTT with univalent universes and
HITs) and an outer theory validating UIP; the outer level is the internalised meta-theory of the
inner. Kovács: the same system is staged compilation with full dependent types, with *strong
conservativity* over the object theory: the meta level adds no equality to it. **The plane.** The
outer level is where every object theory sits as a universe with its own definitional equality
(observational, cubical, intensional with K), privileging none, asserting only UIP; in Deleuze and
Guattari's sense a plane of immanence is a surface on which concepts are laid out with nothing above
them, and Kovács's conservativity is the theorem that the plane does not leak into its universes.
"No single equality is fundamental" becomes a property of the system, not a promise.

**Three bridges**, in three directions: 2LTT, vertical (meta to object; the plane and its
universes); Lambek, interpretive (a theory is a category, a seam a functor with stated properties);
institutions (Goguen–Burstall), horizontal (language to language, ISA to ISA, with a satisfaction
condition: [compiler](02.04.12-compiler-backends-assurance.md)). **Two addresses**: `/fpl/lib/cat` is
an object-level library in the cubical universe; `/fpl/formal/plane` (with `lambek` as first file) is
the outer level: the statement that each universe is a category and each seam a functor, and the
place where cross-universe morphisms are *stated* (R8 with an address). Its first theorems:
conservativity with several universes, the ℤ instance, and the e-graph as truncation of the HIT.
The history: the nPOV supplied the Lambek half (no syntax is fundamental, each calculus is the
internal language of a class of categories, theories of computation are indexed by doctrines: λ
with CCC, linear λ with symmetric monoidal closed, dependent TT with LCCC, HoTT with (∞,1)-topoi,
effects with Kleisli and Freyd categories). Taken: ETCS replaces ZF as a model-giving target; the
synthetic viewpoint is the house style of the category library; linear HoTT is the candidate
universe for a quantum guest. FPL adds where the nLab is thin (concurrency and pi, interaction nets
as machine, complexity, game semantics). **Honest limit**: the literature's 2LTT has one inner
theory; several universes on one plane is this design's extension (the Multiverse is the nearest
precedent) and whether conservativity survives is R3 and R8. A corrected word: the first
formulation said "the object tier is itself a `#lang`"; review showed 2LTT does not license plugging
in arbitrary inner theories, and `#lang` is reserved for front ends with a proven elaboration.

## 6 The rhizome through morphisms

The lattice of theories was a tree for one reason: a single root and one edge kind (extension by a
primitive). The rhizome changes the edge kind, not the nodes. (1) *Morphisms are first-class
outer-level objects*: an interpretation `T ⟶ T′` is a translation of types and terms plus a proof
that it preserves typing and the equational theory (institution morphism or GAT translation), the
content of `/fpl/formal/plane`; C2 gains the judgment beside logical modularity. (2) *Non-inclusion
edges*: CPS, Girard's `!`-embedding, defunctionalisation, the two CBPV translations: lossy or
faithful in stated ways, never extensions. (3) *Multiple edges and cycles*: the obligation is "this
square commutes, up to what". (4) *No privileged root*: CBPV is the largest tree, default and with a
Definition, not the root. (5) *Each edge carries its loss list* (determinism, totality, erasure,
linearity). A term's `&` is already theory-relative; new is *e-class transport along a morphism*,
permitted exactly where the loss list says the declared theory survives, a morphism yielding a
derived hash. R3 restates as consistency per theory, soundness per morphism, global claims only
along paths, a composite inheriting the weakest edge. What is given up is unification of *ontology*;
what is kept is unification of *logistics* (the Dedukti programme, from recall). The plane of
*consistance* (holding the heterogeneous together without a transcendent principle) is the outer
level; immanence is the same plane seen from the universes; *organisation* is the rejected option of
one master theory. *Consistance* is not logical consistency, and the two meet in strong
conservativity. The garden image (gates open in both directions, everyone may plant) is the prose
image for the substrate's introduction; the ladder stays the reviewer artefact in
[substrate liabilities](../02.05-project/notes/02.05.11-substrate-liabilities.md); the toll at the
gate is `+nefas` and the dispensatie in the register ([principles](02.04.07-principles-and-privileges.md)).
