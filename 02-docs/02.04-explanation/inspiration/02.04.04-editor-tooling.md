Question: can the Lark grammar (`01-bootstrap/fpl/grammar.lark`) give us editor tooling for an
accessible IDE in the playground? Answer per item, with what was built under `01-bootstrap/ide/`.
Research claims marked *prior* were not read from a primary source; the rest cite one.

## Why the grammar alone is thin

The grammar is deliberately structural. Every word is one generic `TOKEN`; the shape of a word
(sigil, number, path, modifiers) is the affix pass in `fpl/lex.py`; strings `“ ”` `「 」` and block
comments `⟦ ⟧` are counted by a pre-lexer, and indentation by Lark's `Indenter`. So "derive from the
grammar" really means "derive from `grammar.lark` plus `lex.py`", and the parts neither holds
(what a word *means*) come from the resolver (`desugar.py`).

## Verdicts

| Item | Derivable | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Syntax highlighting | partly | No Lark to TextMate or Monarch converter exists (searched; the nearest, [Monogram](https://github.com/johnsoncodehk/monogram), wants a TypeScript grammar). Monarch and TextMate are regex state machines, not CFGs. Built: terminals read from the grammar through Lark and shapes from `lex.py`; the tokenizer states are hand-written. |
| Semantic highlighting | yes (mechanism), resolver for meaning | [LSP 3.17 semanticTokens](https://microsoft.github.io/language-server-protocol/specifications/lsp/3.17/specification/#textDocument_semanticTokens): legend plus 5-integer relative encoding. A binder vs a use is not in the grammar; `desugar.definition` knows it. |
| LSP server | yes, cheap | [pygls](https://pygls.readthedocs.io/en/latest/) 2.x. Diagnostics are the interpreter's own `FplError(span, message)`; no new analysis. |
| tree-sitter | partly | No converter (searched). `grammar.js` hand-ported (about 60 lines); indentation and counted pairs need an external scanner ([docs](https://tree-sitter.github.io/tree-sitter/creating-parsers/4-external-scanners.html)), about 150 lines of C here. Frames and cells are not nodes: tree-sitter rules may not match empty, and an empty frame is legal FPL. |

## What was built

- `ide/gen_highlight.py` writes `assets/fpl-language.js` (Monaco Monarch, wired into
  `assets/fpl-playground.js`, language id `fpl`, theme `fpl-dark`) and `ide/fpl.tmLanguage.json`
  (VS Code, Shiki, linguist). Regenerate after changing the grammar or `lex.py`.
- `ide/analysis.py` (diagnostics, semantic tokens, LSP encoding; no LSP dependency, so Pyodide can
  load it) and `ide/lsp.py` (pygls: didOpen/didChange diagnostics, semanticTokens/full).
  `ide/smoke_lsp.py` drives it over stdio.
- `ide/tree-sitter-fpl/` (`grammar.js`, `src/scanner.c`, `queries/highlights.scm`).

## Measurements (31 `.fpl` files under `03-fpl/` and `learn/`)

- TextMate (vscode-textmate, Oniguruma): 13092 non-space characters, 0 left unscoped.
- Monarch (`monarchCompile` of monaco-editor 0.52.2, rules run by a small runner): 0 unclassified;
  per-class counts (848 identifier, 97 sigil, 90 number, 31 path) equal TextMate's. The runner is
  not Monaco's own tokenizer; the browser behaviour is unchecked.
- Lark parses 31 of 31; tree-sitter parses 31 of 31 without ERROR or MISSING, and the word count
  agrees on all 31 (`ide/measure_parity.py`). Negative controls `[ 1 2` and `a ]` are refused.
- LSP smoke: `[ 1 2` gives `[ never closed`, a good example gives no diagnostics and tokens.
- `pytest ide`: 35 pass.

## Not done, and cost estimates (*prior*)

- Browser LSP: run `ide/analysis.py` in Pyodide behind Monaco providers (about a day); pygls has
  experimental Pyodide support.
- Hover, go to definition, completion: half a day each from `Catalog.resolve`.
- Block comments and strings with `⟨ ⟩` islands are one token in tree-sitter; islands are not parsed.
- Monarch tokenizes `;` note continuation lines per line; the multi-line NOTE token is not modelled.
- Recommendation: Monarch plus in-page semantic tokens first; tree-sitter only for incremental or
  structural editing, since it is a second parser to keep in step with Lark.
