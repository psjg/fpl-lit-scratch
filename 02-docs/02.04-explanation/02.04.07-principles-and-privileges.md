# 02.04.07 2026-09-27 — Principles, holes and privileges

Five standing principles rank above individual decisions in the
[substrate](02.04.04-substrate.md): when a decision conflicts with one, the principle wins or the
conflict is recorded. They share one mechanism, the *hole*: everything the theory forbids and the
practitioner sometimes needs is a capability the operator holds or withholds, and every loosening
leaves a named, queryable gap. This page states the principles, the loosening machinery (`sorry`,
the keyring, the dispensatie) and profiles (`under`), the unit in which loosening is granted.

## 1 The principles

**P1. Static assurance is emancipation.** The reason FPL is grounded in mechanisable formal
methods is not correctness for its own sake: a hard guarantee frees the attention its absence would
consume. What the checker carries, the operator does not have to carry (edge cases, aliasing,
who-can-call-what, cleanup). The freed capacity is the point; the language's users are *operators*,
and the platform owes them the largest static guarantee it can give by default.

**P2. Safety by default, loosening explicit, and every loosening is a hole.** Where Rust has
`unsafe { }`, FPL has a typed hole: a region the operator chooses to leave unchecked is wrapped in
`_` and discharged with `sorry` (Lean's word; Coq's `admit`). The hole is a first-class object in
the log, so "where are the gaps" is a query and therefore a task list; the residual still builds,
with the discharged obligation as a blame-carrying dynamic check or a recorded trust decision
([contracts](02.04.11-contracts-cost-totality.md)). This is gradual typing (Siek–Taha) with blame
(Wadler–Findler) and the gradual guarantee, with the known cost (checks at the boundary, Takikawa
et al.) localised to the holes instead of spread everywhere. "Dunder and move on" is allowed:
exporting everything is an explicit act that leaves a hole saying *facet not designed*. Loosening
is a capability: operators hold `sorry`; automated agents whose slop must be bounded do not.
Dynamic languages are not the enemy; a ball of mud is sometimes the right tool, and the platform
must let an operator build one, visibly.

Concretely: a loosened region is a non-empty hole `_` discharged by `sorry`; the discharged
obligation becomes a blame-carrying check at the hole's boundary, or an `assume` recorded in the
log; the residual builds either way; holes are queryable; `sorry` is a capability. Grades interact:
a loosened region may not drop a linear value or duplicate a unique one silently, because
loosening the *proof* obligations must not loosen resource safety (the Rust `unsafe` lesson: there
memory safety inside `unsafe` is the programmer's; here it stays the checker's). The glyph for the
hole is `_` (settled 2026-09-27; `?` is unwrap) and `_ [ sorry ]` is the loosening at a point.

**P3. Time is causality.** Time in FPL is the order of the event log (Lamport 1978): every clock,
logical or wall, is a capability whose readings are events, so time is discrete by design. This
buys deterministic replay, provenance, time travel, and clock-extent rights decidable against the
log. It costs no native continuous-time semantics. That costs automatic differentiation nothing
(autodiff differentiates the function a program computes over the reals, not the time in which it
runs; a physics simulation steps discretely and computes smooth functions per step). It costs
*continuous-time models*: ODEs, hybrid systems, dense deadlines. These are libraries with a stated
seam (hybrid coalgebras for dynamics, diffeological spaces for differentiability), and the price a
simulation pays, an integrator and its discretisation error, must be visible (interval arithmetic,
verified numerics; open). The chain rule needs smoothness a lattice does not supply; time-scale
calculus (Hilger) unifies the two with a modified chain rule and is the library's model when both
are needed.

**P4. Compile speed is developer and agent experience, and a design constraint.** Slow compilers
are why people leave Haskell and why Go exists; the bar is Blow's order of magnitude (a million
lines a second on old hardware) for the mandatory work. The mandatory phases (parse and arity,
stack effects, grades, capabilities and extents, hashing) are each linear and local; everything
expensive (dependent elaboration, e-graph optimisation, proofs, staging) is pay-for-what-you-use,
memoised per definition by identity and hash, or asynchronous. Skipping a phase is not a flag but a
hole: `../adyton/unchecked` is a dispensatie that lets a build run unverified and records that it
did. The phase ladder and the dev/release split are in
[compiler and backends](02.04.12-compiler-backends-assurance.md). A speed budget is a done-when of
B0 and C1 ([work graph](02.04.04-substrate.md)).

**P5. Open texture is first-class: a small closed core is the flag, the penumbra stays open by
name, and closing is precedent.** Hart's point about rules applies to a language definition: every
rule has a core of settled meaning and a penumbra where someone must decide, and the penumbra is
not a defect but the only way a rule survives cases it did not foresee. The tension between closing
(critical mass needs a flag) and staying open (a Python 2 to 3 corner is a closed penumbra) is
governed through Hart's secondary rules made explicit. *Recognition*: what counts as FPL is the
Definition of the named centre plus the hash, small, canonical, the one thing not revised lightly.
The flag: *name the hub and lay it as low as it goes; exit is priced, never forbidden.* *Change*: a
point leaves the penumbra as a row in the decisions table, logged, with dependencies named; the
table is case law, not statute, a row binding as far as its dependencies reach and open to being
overruled (the retroactivity of the task graph applied to the language). *Adjudication*: whoever
plants a tree decides the case that tree raises; there is no central judge. The mechanism is P2's
applied to the Definition: every undecided point is a named hole with three fields, what depends on
it, the default in force meanwhile, and who may close it and how (a tree, a decision row, a proof).
Not deciding is then a recorded act with a stated boundary, and the cost of reopening is read off
the first field.

### 1.1 The first four named holes

All in the type-theoretic penumbra and closable later without touching the core.

| Hole | Statement | Depends | Default | Closes by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| H1 | the inner identity type is Martin-Löf's J only: no K and no UIP at object level (K lives on the strict meta level) | D3, R3, `CBPV.agda` (flag `--without-K` from the first line) | J | a proof that adding K is wanted, or never |
| H2 | univalence is not an axiom of the language but an obligation a universe may carry (C2's universes are first-class), cubical computation the tree that discharges it | H1, the third identity of types beside stored and behavioural | no universe claims it | a tree declaring its universe univalent; instance: the univalent category library ([meta level](02.04.15-meta-level-and-plane.md)) |
| H3 | the h-level of the default tier is unstated; that the total pure fragment is a world of h-sets is a derived fact when proven, never a rule | H1 | none | a theorem |
| H4 | grades and identity stay orthogonal until linear HoTT is more than research | C2 grades, H1 | orthogonal | a proof |

One structural consequence: the design already uses category theory unformalised (seams are
functors, full and faithful their properties, the garden a category of theories), so `category/` is
data with laws as obligations and `seam` is typed by it; the language describes its own garden in
its own library and there are not two notions of functor. Univalent categories follow from H2 for
free ([the plane](02.04.15-meta-level-and-plane.md)).

## 2 The keyring: every forbidden power is a capability

One rule for everything the theory forbids and the practitioner sometimes needs: it is a
capability the operator holds or withholds, visible in the authority graph
([capabilities](02.04.08-capabilities-effects-failure.md)) either way. Nothing is a prohibition;
each is a key, and holding a key is a fact about the program, not the language.

| Key | Power |
| --- | --- |
| `../meta/run` | arbitrary code at compile time (Jai's `#run`), including loading anything |
| `../meta/reflect` | fexpr behaviour: inspecting quotations as syntax |
| `../meta/callcc` | reifying the whole stack (delimited continuations already exist as handlers) |
| `../meta/goto` | a jump out of a frame at the surface; join points are native on the residual, a surface jump breaks a frame's extent and must be labelled |
| `sorry` | discharging an obligation unproved |
| level-0 grants (`bind` at run time) | dynamic authority; withholding it gives a fully static authority graph |
| dynamic-scope installation | `+ctx/…` handler installation ([capabilities](02.04.08-capabilities-effects-failure.md)) |
| `../adyton/unchecked`, `+nefas` | skipping typechecking for a whole program or word, recorded as a dispensatie |
| `../adyton/reflect` | run-time symbolic computation ([meta level](02.04.15-meta-level-and-plane.md)) |
| float-training dispensatie | float training as a non-replayable region ([numbers](02.04.13-numbers-ml-library.md)) |
| unhygienic macro scope | a macro writing into the caller's scope |

## 3 Profiles are privileges

Perl's `use strict`, Haskell's `{-# LANGUAGE #-}`, Python's `from __future__`, Rust's editions: one
line at the top saying under which regime the rest reads. Here it is not a construct but a mount:
`#ML under` at the top of a file, program or workbook is `../profiles/ML bind` at that scope's
root, logged. A profile directory holds:

1. a **keyring**, the dispensaties it opens (`ML`: float as default representation, vendor jets, `+div` in training loops, contracts off for smooth words; `gamedev`: arenas, hot reload, machine-tier `goto`; `nefas`: everything);
2. **defaults**, which representation a literal gets (float under `ML`, decimal fixed under `finance`), whether `+fail` cascades or is silent;
3. **sugar**, the vocabulary (JAX and NumPy names under `ML`, Jai's under `gamedev`);
4. a **view** (the S3 views of [syntax rationale](02.04.05-syntax-rationale.md)): `under python` is a Python-shaped surface plus the dynamic dispensaties a Python programmer expects, `under lisp` a prefix surface; the ABT and the log are the same underneath.

Three properties beyond a pragma. The profile is one node in the authority graph ("this file runs
under ML", not three hundred holes) and part of the interface hash, since a word compiled under
`ML` has different obligations. Tightening is a diff: from `under ML` to `under ML/reproducible`
(integer default, no float dispensatie, contracts on), what appears is exactly the list of `sorry`s
that were real, as tasks. And the existing GPU fleet is covered by the profile, not the core: `ML`
brings the CUDA backend and cuBLAS jets, the reproducible path stays opt-in inside it.

**Names.** In the juridical vocabulary the document already uses (dispensatie, privilege, nefas): a
profile is a **privilege** (historically an exception granted to a town or guild on the common
law); the strictest regime, against which all holes are counted, is **`common`** (the *ius
commune*); the vocabulary in scope before anything is said is **`prelude`**. The keyword is
**`under`**, with `use` as an alias the printer canonicalises. First privileges: `ML`,
`ML/reproducible`, `gamedev`, `finance`, `python`, `lisp`, `nefas` (initially only `common`, `ML`
and `ML/reproducible`).

**Spelling (syntax author's verdict).** `under` is spelled like `bind`: postfix with a symbol
argument, `#ML under`, effect `under : profile --`, scope-local, so at the top of a file it governs
the rest of that scope. Wrapping a passage is putting the passage in a scope (`[ … ] !` or a headed
block), not a second word and not an optional arity. A *view is a directory entry*: `ML/view/read`
and `ML/view/print` are quotations, the printer law is a contract on the pair (`⊨ read ∘ print =
id`), and `read` must be a total recogniser (LangSec). So `under python` and `under lisp` are data,
and the FML profile ([FML](../02.03-reference/02.03.04-fml.md)) is the same mechanism. A
tensor-comprehension view (`under sac`) is accepted as a view when comprehension binders are `→i`
binders in the ABT and the view is a bijection onto the fragment the combinators express; outside
it the printer falls back to postfix. Index origin is a privilege default
([numbers](02.04.13-numbers-ml-library.md)).

### 3.1 The musical reading

The rule that keeps a metaphor honest: every musical term must name a mechanism that already
exists. Under that rule: **score** is the code, `&`, what the work *is*; **performance** a run, the
log, `$`; **recording** replay; **another take** a fork; **tonic** `common`; **key** a privilege;
**modulation** a privilege for a *passage* (`under ML` around a block and back: `unsafe { }` woven
through a program, better because every modulation is a logged event and an authority-graph node);
**resolution** tightening, with the register as the list of what still sounds off; **distance to
the tonic** a metric on the register (how many keys a passage holds, weighted; `ML/reproducible` is
nearer `common` than `ML`; the privileges form a circle-of-fifths-like order); **caesura** the
syntax author's bar; **prelude** what sounds before the piece. Two things the reading adds that the
juridical one did not: privileges may scope a passage, not only head a file, and distance to the
tonic is computable, P1's assurance as one number per passage. The philosophy is Goodman
(*Languages of Art*): music is allographic, the work is the score and every compliant performance
an instance, and code is allographic in exactly that sense, the log being the performance; content
and history (C1 and C6) are Goodman's distinction. Keywords stay juridical; the reading is
musical.

Done-when for the `profiles/` node: `#ML under` mounts a profile and appears as one node in the
authority graph; the same corpus checked under `ML` and `ML/reproducible` yields a computed diff of
obligations equal to the holes an operator would have to discharge; the distance-to-`common` metric
is computed for one passage; one view (`python`) round-trips.
