# DI-0066 The dialectic must be in the page more

## His words

> er zit een dialectiek in de tekst dat is fijn die moet er meer inzitten

## Context

The general note of the annotation round of 2026-10-28. Claim, objection and resolution per
decision. A mechanical form of it: every decision on a page names the alternative it rejected
and what that costs (the ADR shape, at page scale). Related to DI-0065, which would give each
step of that argument a number.

## Options and outcome

Open. Proposal; not implemented.
